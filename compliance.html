Destination rules come first

Before quoting we check the product against the import rules of the destination country and the export policy of India at that time. Some products may be restricted or prohibited for some destinations, and some, such as rice, are exported only when Indian policy permits.

Chemicals

Certain substances, including nonylphenol ethoxylates, are restricted in the European Union and other markets. Hazardous goods are packed, labelled and shipped under the applicable dangerous-goods rules.

Documents

Certificates of origin, phytosanitary certificates, SDS, TDS and COA are arranged where they apply and where the producer or authority issues them.
