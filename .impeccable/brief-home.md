# Homepage (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Code-led (no image generation available on this machine).

## Brief (shape)

- Job and audience: overseas importers and procurement teams arrive with a product in mind; they must confirm GoodBiz sources it and send a specific enquiry.
- Outcome: find a product in seconds (search or category rows), add it to an enquiry or request chemical pricing, submit one enquiry. Proof is honest only: portfolio facts, named products, open-licence photos of the trade.
- Selected direction: user-pinned reference ofbusiness.com, layout and bright look, recoloured toward GoodBiz navy and teal; no copying of its logo, words or photos. Concept roll skipped because the direction is pinned.
- Scope: homepage rebuilt; legal pages inherit tokens. Keep data models, enquiry list, chemical catalogue, forms, credits.
- Anti-goals: fake prices or live price ticker, fake stats, partner logos, eyebrow pills, gradient text, invented sub-categories.
- States: product search with no results, product without a photo, long chemical names, invalid/failed form submission, empty enquiry list.
- Layout: gradient header + enquiry ticker; split hero (copy + search left, port photo fading right) with three action cards; five category rows (photo tile + six product thumbnails + fact strip + expandable full list); facts band; chemical catalogue panel with group tabs and pricing form; tabbed five-step process; reasons carousel; photo gallery; markets; quote panel; link-column gradient footer. Mobile: rows stack, thumbnails scroll horizontally, header collapses to menu.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A search-led sourcing desk. The buyer types a product and lands on its row with an enquiry action; the page refuses the editorial poster and the generic hero-plus-three-cards template by making the working search and the category rows the page.

OWN-WORLD: White and cool mist grounds; a teal-to-blue gradient owns the header, facts band, process panel and footer; deep navy ink; Cabinet Grotesk 700/800 headlines in two solid tones (teal, navy), Switzer text; 14px-radius rows and thumbnails with soft offset shadows; pill chips for quick searches; real photographs in every row tile.

STORY: Understand five sectors in one glance, believe the desk is specific (named products, origins, documents, rules), act by searching, adding products and sending one enquiry.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Gradient header with wordmark, Categories menu, Chemicals, How we work, About, white "Request a quote" button; ticker of products open for enquiry beneath. Left 55%: two-line headline "Indian Sourcing. / Global Opportunities.", one supporting line, a 64px search field with button, five quick chips. Right: JNPT port photograph fading into white toward the copy. Three action cards overlap the hero's bottom edge.

FORM: user-pinned reference (ofbusiness.com layout and bright look); the concept-seed roll was skipped because a pinned direction beats the roll, so no seed reference exists for this surface. Signature interaction: live product search across all 49 products with sector, origin and an Add/Request pricing action in each result. Motion grammar: ticker drift, results dropdown fade-slide, row thumbnails lift on hover, tab panel crossfade, carousel slide; all with reduced-motion fallbacks.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
