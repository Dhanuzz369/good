# Design System: GoodBiz Global

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Bright Sourcing Desk"**

A working trade counter, not a brochure. The page is white and cool mist, lit by one teal-to-blue gradient on the header bar only. Deep panels (process, footer, range CTA) are flat navy; the facts strip is mist. Content sits on white, with hairline-bordered cards: range cards, a chemical table, forms. Search and enquiry controls are the heroes. Persuasion comes after them.

The density is high, like a marketplace: product names, origins and actions packed into scannable rows. The layout follows the user-pinned reference (ofbusiness.com), recoloured to GoodBiz teal, blue and navy, without its logo, wording or photos. Real, credited photographs of the trade fill every category tile, the hero and the gallery. Where no honest photograph exists, a sector-tinted line icon stands in. A photo is never faked.

Headlines are set in Cabinet Grotesk in two solid tones: navy or ink for the statement and teal for the second phrase. Running text, tables and forms use Switzer with tabular numerals. Every interactive surface is pill-shaped or softly rounded, and every action has a 44px floor.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White and mist grounds. The teal-to-blue gradient belongs to the header bar alone.
- One-tone grotesk section headlines in ink. Only the hero headline carries a teal phrase. No gradient text.
- Pill controls: buttons, chips, tabs, search, add toggles.
- 14px rounded cards and 22px rows with soft, diffuse navy-tinted shadows.
- Real photography in every row tile. Line icons on a 24px grid as the only fallback.
- Blue drives actions. Teal marks brand, selection and enquiry state.

## Colors

A cool, bright palette: a white and mist base, a teal and blue pair, and deep navy ink.

### Primary
- **Harbour Teal** (teal): brand tone. Used for the second phrase of two-tone headlines, active tabs, the added state of the enquiry toggle, focus halos on inputs, the text caret, selection, checkbox accents, small line icons in fact strips, and the 3px top rule on reason slides.
- **Deep Harbour Teal** (teal-600): teal text on light tints (chip hover, add toggle, ticker label) where the base teal would be too light.
- **Teal Wash** (teal-50): tint behind the add toggle, icon wells, category fact strips, search-match highlights and selected chemical rows.

### Secondary
- **Trade Blue** (blue): the action colour. Used for the primary button fill, links, the focus outline, white-button text, round carousel buttons on hover, and the enquiry count.
- **Pressed Blue** (blue-600): hover state of the brand button and text on blue-wash buttons.
- **Blue Wash** (blue-50): soft buttons, the "go" disc on action cards, and white-button hover.

### Tertiary
- **Ledger Navy** (navy): hero headline, slide headlines, chemical group headings, the mobile enquiry bar, and the fallback ground behind category photos.

### Neutral
- **Paper White** (white): page ground and every card, form and dropdown surface.
- **Cool Mist** (mist): hero ground, inset panels (spec, "also available", empty quote state, enquiry items), hover rows in dropdowns, and image placeholders.
- **Hairline** (line): 1px borders on cards, chips, tabs, rows, the ticker underline and table dividers.
- **Strong Hairline** (line-strong): form-field strokes, search-field hover, empty-state icons.
- **Ink** (ink): body text and section headlines.
- **Ink Secondary** (ink-2): leads, field labels, chip and tab text, and secondary copy.
- **Muted** (muted): hints, counts, small captions, and the origin text in ticker items.
- **On-Brand / On-Brand Soft** (on-brand, on-brand-2): text on the gradient fields. The soft tone is for supporting lines.

### Gradients (sidecar)
- **Brand Field** (`--grad`, 100deg, teal-600 to #0f5f8f to blue): the header bar only.
- **Deep Field** (`--navy`, flat): process panel, range CTA and footer. The primary action card is flat teal-600.

### Named Rules
**The One Gradient Rule.** The teal-to-blue gradient appears once, on the header bar. Never on text, panels, cards or bands.

**The Blue Acts, Teal Belongs Rule.** Primary actions (submit, request a quote, search) are blue. Teal marks brand, selection and enquiry membership (active tab, added product). A teal primary button would blur the two.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Cabinet Grotesk (with Switzer, Helvetica Neue, Arial)
**Body Font:** Switzer (with Helvetica Neue, Arial, system-ui)

**Character:** Cabinet Grotesk is a confident grotesk with sharp terminals, used at 700–800 with tight tracking for statements. Switzer is a neutral, legible workhorse for the product names, units and form labels that buyers scan, with tabular numerals on by default.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp 2.375–4.5rem, 1.02, -0.035em): the hero headline only, in navy with a teal second line.
- **Headline** (800, clamp 1.875–3rem, 1.1, -0.025em): section titles in ink with a teal tone phrase. Legal page titles use this size.
- **Panel headline** (800, clamp 1.75–2.5rem): process step titles on the gradient, and reason slide titles (clamp 1.5–2rem, navy, max 14ch).
- **Title** (700, clamp 1.1875–1.375rem): form titles, footer title, legal h2. Category tile titles are 1.4375rem at 800, and action-card titles are 1.1875rem at 700.
- **Lead** (400, clamp 1.0625–1.25rem, 1.55): section and hero supporting lines, ink-2, max 54–58ch.
- **Body** (400, clamp 1–1.0625rem, 1.55): running text with tabular numerals.
- **Label** (600, 0.875rem): buttons (0.9375rem), chips, field labels, product names in thumbnails (0.9375rem), and captions. Small meta text is 0.8125rem.

### Named Rules
**The One-Accent Headline Rule.** Section titles are one tone (ink). Only the hero headline sets a phrase in teal (`.tone`). Never gradient text, never a third colour.

**The Sentence-Case Rule.** Labels, buttons and column headings are sentence case at normal or near-normal tracking (at most 0.02em). Nothing is set in tracked uppercase.

## Layout

The content column is 1280px max, with fluid side gutters of clamp(16px, 3vw, 40px). Full-bleed bands (header, ticker, facts, footer) pad to the same column using `max(gutter, (100% - max) / 2)`. Sections are separated by clamp(64px, 8vw, 112px). Section heads are a grid of title plus lead, capped at 760px. A row variant puts a control group (carousel arrows) on the right.

The header is sticky: a 72px gradient bar over a 40px white ticker that collapses on scroll. The hero is split: copy and a 64px search field in the left ~660px, and a photograph masked to fade into mist on the right. Three action cards overlap the hero's bottom edge by 48px. Category rows use a 248px photo tile plus a body with six thumbnails (3 below 1180px). Two-column panels use 7/5 or 5/7 splits (chemicals, about, quote).

Responsive behaviour: the navigation collapses to a menu below 1080px. Panels go single-column below 1020–860px. Below 640px, category thumbnails become a horizontal snap scroller (42% columns), tab sets scroll horizontally past the gutter, the chemical table becomes stacked rows, and the quote panel goes full-bleed with square corners. Below 760px, a fixed pill-shaped enquiry bar in navy appears at the bottom.

Inner rhythm uses 8–22px gaps (8, 10, 12, 14, 16, 18, 20). Card padding is 14–20px, and panel padding is fluid, clamp(20–24px to 32–56px).

## Elevation & Depth

The system is lightly lifted. Surfaces sit on soft, diffuse shadows tinted with ink (`rgb(11 29 46)`), never pure black and never hard offsets. The default lift is for cards and rows at rest. The higher lift is for floating things (search field, dropdowns, mega menu, action cards, quote form, mobile bar). Depth also comes from tonal layering: white cards on mist, never a card inside another card.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Rest** (`box-shadow: 0 1px 2px rgb(11 29 46 / 0.06), 0 4px 14px rgb(11 29 46 / 0.06)`): cards and category rows at rest.
- **Float** (`box-shadow: 0 2px 6px rgb(11 29 46 / 0.06), 0 14px 34px rgb(11 29 46 / 0.12)`): search field, results dropdown, mega menu, action cards, quote form, mobile bar, skip link.

### Named Rules
**The Soft Lift Rule.** Shadows are diffuse and ink-tinted. Hover raises a card by translating it 3px, deepening its shadow and zooming its photo slightly (1.04–1.06). Hard offset shadows are not part of this world.

## Shapes

Shapes are soft and rounded throughout, with no square corners on the main page. Corners come in four steps: 8px for fields, icon wells, dropdown rows and inset strips; 14px for cards, panels, the chemical table card and gallery tiles; 22px for category rows, the about card, the process panel and the quote panel; and a full pill for every button, chip, tab, toggle, the search field and badges. Round 44px discs are for carousel arrows, with 32px discs for "go" arrows on action cards. Photos are always clipped to their container's radius. Borders are 1px hairlines. The only heavier stroke is the 3px teal top rule on reason slides.

## Components

### Buttons
Pill-shaped and confident, with a 44–48px touch floor.
- **Shape:** full pill (999px).
- **Brand:** blue fill, white text, 600 at 0.9375rem, padding 10px 22px, min-height 48px, with a soft blue glow (`0 4px 14px rgb(25 87 166 / 0.28)`). Hover darkens to pressed blue.
- **White:** for use on the gradient. White fill with blue text, and a blue-wash hover.
- **Soft:** blue-wash fill with pressed-blue text. Hover inverts to a blue fill with white text. Used for per-row "Request pricing".
- **Small:** min-height 44px, padding 8px 16px, 0.875rem.
- **States:** press scales to 0.97. Loading appends a 16px spinning ring. Focus is a 2.5px blue outline at a 3px offset (white on gradient fields).
- **Round:** 44px white disc with a hairline border. Hover fills it blue. Used for carousel arrows.

### Add-to-Enquiry Toggle (signature)
A teal-wash pill (min 44px) with teal-600 text. Hover and the added state fill it teal with white text. Every product, wherever it appears (thumbnail, search result, also-available list), carries this toggle or a soft "Request pricing" pill. Adding a product sends a small teal fly-chip to the header count, which bumps.

### Chips
- **Style:** white pill with a hairline border, ink-2 text, 600 at 0.875rem, min 44px.
- **Hover:** teal border, teal-wash fill, teal-600 text. Used for quick searches under the hero field.

### Tabs
- **Style:** the same pill as chips, at 0.9375rem with padding 8px 18px, and an optional 18px icon and muted count.
- **Active:** solid teal fill and border, with white text. Used for chemical group filters and process steps. On mobile, the set scrolls horizontally.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 14px for cards and action cards, 22px for category rows and large panels.
- **Background:** white on white or mist. Inset panels inside cards are mist (spec, also-available) or teal-wash (fact strip).
- **Shadow Strategy:** Rest at rest, Float for floating elements (see Elevation).
- **Border:** 1px hairline.
- **Internal Padding:** 14–20px for cards, and fluid up to 44–56px for gradient panels.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** white background, 1px strong-hairline stroke, 8px radius, min-height 48px, padding 11px 14px, 1rem text. Labels are 600 at 0.875rem in ink-2, and hints are 0.8125rem in muted.
- **Hover:** the stroke darkens to ink-2.
- **Focus:** the stroke turns teal with a 4px teal halo (`0 0 0 4px rgb(10 127 114 / 0.16)`) and no outline.
- **Error:** the stroke turns error red on an error-wash background, with a 600 error message at 0.8125rem below.

### Search (signature)
A 64px white pill with the Float shadow, a 22px search icon inset at 20px, and a brand button docked inside on the right. Focus turns the stroke teal and adds a halo. Results drop 10px below in a 14px-radius white panel with the Float shadow, fading and sliding in. Each row has a 48px thumbnail, the product name with teal-wash match highlighting, sector and origin in muted text, and an add or pricing action. There is an inline 52px variant without the shadow. On mobile, the field is 56px and the docked button is hidden.

### Category Row (signature)
A white 22px row with a hairline border and the Rest shadow. On the left, a 248px photo tile under a navy scrim, with the white title and lead in the top-left. On the right, six 4:3 thumbnails (10px radius) with name and add toggle, a teal-wash fact strip with teal line icons, and a white "View all" pill that expands the full two-column list.

### Navigation
- **Header bar:** 72px gradient. The wordmark is Cabinet Grotesk 800 at 1.625rem with a 500-weight second word and a 0.75rem tagline. - **Division brand lockup:** the division's icon in a rounded square of its accent colour beside "GoodBiz" (small, Switzer) over the division name (Cabinet Grotesk 800). ## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** set every interactive control to at least 44px tall, and use the pill radius for buttons, chips, tabs and toggles.
- **Do** use blue for actions and teal for brand, selection and enquiry state (The Blue Acts, Teal Belongs Rule).
- **Do** set headlines in Cabinet Grotesk 700/800 with negative tracking, in two solid tones at most.
- **Do** use the gradient only as a full-bleed band or large panel, with white or on-brand-2 text on it and white focus rings.
- **Do** put a real, credited photograph in every product or category tile. Where none exists, use the sector-tinted line icon from the single 24px, 1.75-stroke icon set.
- **Do** give every motion (ticker drift, dropdown pop, card lift, tab crossfade, reveal) a reduced-motion fallback.

## Motion

Tokens: `--ease-out: cubic-bezier(0.23, 1, 0.32, 1)` for anything entering or responding, `--ease-in-out: cubic-bezier(0.77, 0, 0.175, 1)` for on-screen movement, `--ease-drawer: cubic-bezier(0.32, 0.72, 0, 1)` for sheets. Durations: press 140ms, hover/colour 160ms, dropdowns and panels 200ms in and 150ms out, mobile enquiry bar 280ms in and 180ms out, entrances 500ms. All rules live in `src/styles/motion.css`.

- Only `transform` and `opacity` move; the category "View all" accordion (grid rows, 180–260ms) is the one sanctioned layout animation. No `transition: all`, no `scale(0)`, no ease-in.
- Every pressable control scales to 0.97 on `:active`; cards to 0.985.
- Hover travel (card lift, image zoom, arrow nudge) only under `(hover: hover) and (pointer: fine)`.
- Dropdown, phone menu, search results and mobile bar are interruptible transitions with exit states (never `display` toggles), so rapid toggling retargets instead of restarting.
- One authored entrance: the hero headline, lead, search, chips and action cards rise 14px with a 70ms stagger on first load. Scroll reveals only on category rows and brand cards (60ms stagger, capped at 240ms).
- Never animated: chemical filtering and search typing, keyboard tab switches, result-list updates.
- Reduced motion keeps opacity and colour feedback, drops all travel, stops the ticker and banner autoplay.
- **Do** mark unconfirmed facts with the amber "to be confirmed" pill instead of omitting the field silently.

### Don't:
- **Don't** use gradient text, a third headline colour, or the gradient on small elements.
- **Don't** use hard offset shadows or pure-black shadows. Lift is soft and ink-tinted.
- **Don't** add eyebrow pills or tracked-uppercase kickers above section titles. Titles stand alone, with a lead.
- **Don't** show prices, price tickers, invented statistics or partner logos. The ticker lists products open for enquiry, and the facts band lists verifiable portfolio counts.
- **Don't** use square-cornered cards on the main page. The only square edge is the full-bleed quote panel on mobile.
